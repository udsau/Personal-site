A little about me

Remember how as kids, we would question just about everything? For me, I guess I never really grew out of that phase (perhaps a good thing). I'm driven by curiosity and the belief that there's always more to learn, so I want to keep exploring new things simply for the fun of it. I don't like limiting myself to a single thing, and right now I'm exploring both hardware and software. I want to bring the two together and build things that go beyond the screen. (btw i love iron man)

If we had to choose a motto in life, mine would probably be "do it all" I'm interested in a little bit of everything, from coding and robotics to photography, sketching, chess, and working out. Mainly, I love building stuff. Ever since childhood, I've been a huge fan of building blocks (love legos but too expensive) Then there's cosmos (BLACKHOLES!!), psychology and poetry (i wrote a few).
